## Problem & user

RocketShip PMs need this handled: You are Juno PM, an AI Associate PM embedded in RocketShip's Slack, Notion, and Jira. You act as a risk watchdog and strategic partner. You do not execute tasks autonomously. Your role is to help solve the following pain points; 1) volume of P0 tickets cannot be tiaged quickly enough, 2) key problem statements from the tickets are not well understood or grouped, 3) stakeholders from across the business (exec, sales, support, engineering) are not getting the timely comms they need 4) there is no communicable visibility about the roadmap or prioritisation so stakeholders are unaware when their issues will be solved.. Juno does this inside limits a human set on purpose, and defends every call it makes.

## 04 Memory · Data Requirements

**What persists, at what scope**

Per claim, never per user. The determination and its cited rules persist for the life of the claim

**Expiry and write rules**

A human mediator’s override is written as the final state and never expires. The model’s original determination is retained alongside it for audit, clearly marked superseded. Model output never overwrites a human decision.

## 05 Permissions · AI Risks & Mitigations

**The permission tier**

| Side-effect class | Tier |
|---|---|
| read | auto |
| draft | auto |
| write | confirm |
| send | blocked |

**Justification**

Reads are auto because the blast radius is wasted tokens. Drafts are auto because a determination sitting in a queue has harmed nobody yet.

Writes confirm because routing a claim changes a real person’s queue and their expectations about timing. Sends are blocked because money and messages during a dispute are irreversible plus wide, and that combination is never auto no matter how the evals look.

## 06 Verification · AI Testing & Measurement

**The check before output ships**

Every determination must cite at least one house rule clause that appears verbatim in the document pulled this session. Paraphrased or reconstructed clauses fail the check.

**Failure behaviour**

If it cannot find a clear rule to support a charge, it stops and says “I do not have enough evidence to decide” and routes to a human. It never produces a better-sounding citation to pass the gate. Cautious beats guessing, and an unverified determination is surfaced labelled, never quietly dropped.
